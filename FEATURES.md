# Isaac Search Engine — User Features Guide

This document provides a complete overview of all user-facing features and capabilities available in **Isaac Search Engine**.

---

## 1. Web & Image Search (`Search` Tab)

### A. Smart Search & Query Assistance
- **Relevance-Ranked Web Search**: Searches across web page titles, summaries, full article text, authors, and topic tags to surface the most relevant results first.
- **Relevance Score Breakdown**: Click the relevance badge on any result to view why it matched your search, including keyword matches in the title, summary, and body text.
- **Instant Autocomplete & Suggestions**: Displays real-time keyword suggestions and recent searches as you type, with full keyboard arrow navigation (`↑` / `↓` / `Enter`).
- **Did-You-Mean Spellcheck**: Automatically detects common spelling mistakes and offers a one-click correction link.
- **Voice Search**: Click the microphone icon in the search bar to speak your search query hands-free.
- **Search Within Results**: Use the secondary filter bar to narrow down the currently displayed results with live keyword highlighting across titles, summaries, and previews.

### B. Live Web Discovery
- **Automatic Live Web Expansion**: When a topic has only a few saved matches, the search engine automatically fetches fresh results from the live web so you always get comprehensive answers.
- **One-Click Live Refresh**: Click **"Fetch Live"** at any time to pull additional real-time web results for your query.

### C. Search Filters & Sorting
- **Custom Filters**:
  - **Website / Domain Filter**: Limit results to a specific website (for example, `wikipedia.org`).
  - **Minimum Authority / Backlinks**: Filter results by how widely referenced the page is across the web.
  - **Date Range Filter**: Filter results by `Any Time`, `Past 24 Hours`, `Past 7 Days`, `Past 30 Days`, `Past Year`, or a `Custom Date Range`.
- **8 Sorting Options**:
  - Sort results by **Relevance**, **Most Upvoted**, **Newest**, **Oldest**, **Most Referenced (Backlinks)**, **Title (A–Z)**, **Title (Z–A)**, or **Language**.
- **Topic Tag Filter Bar**:
  - Click one or more `#topic` tags above the results to filter pages by subject.
  - Switch between **Match Any Tag** or **Match All Selected Tags** to broaden or narrow your view.

### D. Result Card Tools & Pagination
- **Quick Article Preview**: Click **Preview** on any search result to read its full text, word count, and character count directly on the page without leaving your search results.
- **Community Notes**: Read helpful context notes added by other users on any link, write your own note, or vote on whether existing notes are helpful.
- **Result Actions on Every Card**:
  - **Upvote**: Like helpful pages to boost them when sorting by upvotes.
  - **Save to Collection**: Bookmark any result into a topic folder (or create a new folder on the spot).
  - **Read Aloud**: Listen to any search result read aloud using text-to-speech, with a one-click stop button.
  - **Copy Link**: Copy the page URL to your clipboard with instant visual confirmation.
  - **Share via Email**: Open a pre-filled email draft with the result's title, link, and summary.
  - **Site Preferences Menu (`⋮`)**:
    - *Only show results from this website*
    - *Search again without this website*
    - *Hide this website from all future searches*
    - *Link this page to a Research Project*
- **"Show More" & "Show Less" Results**:
  - Click **Show More** at the bottom of Web Search or Image Search results to load and view additional matches on demand, or click **Show Less** to collapse the list back to the top results.
- **Download Results as PDF**: Export your current search results into a clean, readable PDF document with one click.

### E. Zero-Click Instant Answer Widgets (DuckDuckGo Style)
- **Interactive Weather Widget**: Type `weather`, `weather in Tokyo`, `Paris weather`, or `temperature in London` to view a live weather card complete with temperature, animated condition icon, °C/°F toggle, "feels like" temp, humidity %, wind speed (mph & km/h), precipitation chance, 5-day daily forecast, and quick city switchers.
- **Enhanced Quick Dictionary**: Type `define serendipity`, `meaning of resilience`, `algorithm definition`, or `what does entropy mean` to see authentic pronunciation phonetics, part of speech badge, multiple definitions, example usage, clickable synonym pills, and text-to-speech audio pronunciation.
- **Accurate Unit & Currency Converter**: Type queries like `100 usd to eur`, `50 c in f`, `10 km to miles`, `convert 100 lbs in kg`, or `$100 to €` to view exact mathematical conversions with step-by-step formula breakdown, live FX exchange rate synchronization, interactive dropdowns for unit switching, and a ⇄ Swap button.
- **Dynamic Wikipedia InfoBox with Typo Tolerance**: Type any famous person, scientist, historical figure, or concept (such as `issac newton` [with typo correction], `Albert Einstein`, `Nikola Tesla`, `Marie Curie`, or `Alan Turing`) to view an instant biographical infobox with portrait image, key fact attributes (Born, Died, Known for, Alma mater), speech synthesis narration, and direct Wikipedia article link.
- **Interactive Calculator**: Type math expressions (such as `45 * 12 + 8`, `sqrt(144)`, or `25% of 800`) to open an interactive on-page calculator with display, copy button, and interactive keypad.
- **Visual Color Picker**: Type `color picker` or a hex code like `#3b82f6` to access an interactive color preview with one-click HEX, RGB, and HSL copy buttons.
- **World Time & Clock**: Search `time in Tokyo`, `time in London`, or `time in New York` to see real-time localized clocks, current dates, and timezone indicators.
- **Interactive Stopwatch**: Search `stopwatch` or `timer` to start, pause, and reset an interactive digital stopwatch.

### F. Multi-Media Tabs: Images, Videos & News
- **Image Search & Color Palette Filter**: Browse visual image thumbnails and filter them by dominant colors (`Red`, `Blue`, `Green`, `Teal`, etc.).
- **Dedicated Videos Tab**: Browse video search results with video thumbnail previews, duration badges (`14:28`), creator channels, view counts, and an **Inline Video Player Modal** to watch videos directly inside Isaac Search without opening new tabs.
- **Dedicated News Tab**: Browse real-time news headlines, relative publication times (`2h ago`, `Yesterday`), publisher tags (Reuters, TechCrunch, Hacker News), summary snippets, and read-aloud playback.

### G. SafeSearch Filter Toggle
- **3-Level SafeSearch Control**: Switch between **Strict** (filters adult text, images & videos), **Moderate** (filters explicit multimedia while preserving informative articles), and **Off**.
- **Persistent Preferences**: SafeSearch settings persist across sessions in local storage.

## 2. AI Research & Answer Engine (`Fireplexity` Tab)

- **Instant Cited Summaries**: Ask complex questions in natural language and receive a structured, easy-to-read summary complete with numbered clickable source citations (`[1]`, `[2]`, etc.).
- **Unified Sources, News & Visuals**: View the synthesized answer alongside the original web sources, related news stories, and relevant images in one place.
- **Interactive Stock Charts**: Searching for a public company or stock ticker (such as `NVDA`, `AAPL`, `GOOGL`, `TSLA`, or `MSFT`) automatically displays an interactive financial chart above the summary.
- **Follow-Up Questions & Conversation History**: Continue your research with context-aware follow-up questions, or click any of the suggested follow-up prompts at the end of an answer.

---

## 3. Custom Page Library & Topic Organization (`Discovery & Library` Tab)

- **Add Your Own Web Pages & Images**: Save custom web links, articles, summaries, authors, topic tags, and images into your searchable library so they appear in future searches.
- **Smart Tag Suggestions**: Automatically generate relevant topic tags and summary keywords when adding or editing a saved page.
- **Visual Topic Tag Tree**: Browse all topics in your library using either a collapsible parent-child **Category Tree** or a **Tag Cloud**, and click any tag to view all matching pages.

---

## 4. Interactive Website Connection Map (`Site Graph` Tab)

- **Visual Link Map**: Explore how websites and articles connect to one another through an interactive network map where larger circles represent more widely referenced pages.
- **4 Visual Layouts**:
  - **Freeform Sandbox**: Drag and arrange pages freely on the canvas.
  - **Hub Orbit**: Centers the most Popular/Referenced page with related pages arranged in rings around it.
  - **Starburst**: Arranges pages outward in a radial pattern.
  - **Website Clusters**: Groups pages from the same website together into color-coded regions.
- **4 Color Themes**:
  - Color-code pages on the map by **Website**, **Language**, **Page Status**, or **Importance Tier**.
- **Interactive Map Controls**:
  - Click any page on the map to see all pages linking to and from it.
  - Search and highlight specific pages on the map, zoom and pan smoothly, filter by minimum connections, and toggle page labels on or off.

---

## 5. Bookmarks & Collections (`Collections` Tab)

- **Board & Folder Tree Views**: Organize your saved search results into topic folders using either a visual multi-column **Board View** or a compact **Tree View**.
- **Drag-and-Drop Organization**: Drag folders to prioritize your most important topics, or drag saved bookmarks from one folder to another.
- **Folder Notes**: Keep dedicated summary notes inside each collection folder, automatically saved as you type.
- **Export & Backup Collections**: Export any folder (or multiple folders at once) as a **PDF**, **Markdown (`.md`)**, **CSV spreadsheet**, or **JSON backup** file, and import saved collections at any time.

---

## 6. Research Projects Workspace (`Projects` Tab)

- **Personal Research Workspaces**: Create dedicated projects to organize your reading, tasks, deadlines, and notes for any topic.
- **Ready-Made Project Templates**: Start quickly with built-in checklists for *Web Research*, *Competitive Analysis*, *Literature Review*, or *Topic Exploration*, or build your own checklist from scratch.
- **Tasks & Subtasks with Drag-and-Drop**:
  - Break big goals down into smaller subtasks, check items off to track your progress bar, and drag tasks to reorder your priorities.
  - Filter your checklist by `All`, `Active`, or `Completed` tasks.
- **Connected Bookmarks**: Link relevant Collection folders and saved web pages directly to your project so all your sources stay in one place.
- **Autosaving Research Notebook**: Write and format rich notes in Markdown with live **Write** and **Preview** modes, live word/character counts, and automatic saving.
- **Download Project Briefs**: Export your complete project—including your notes, task progress, and saved sources—as a **Formatted PDF Brief**, **Markdown document**, **CSV**, or **JSON** file.

---

## 7. Personalization, Shortcuts & Privacy

- **Quick Command Bar (`Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K`)**: Quickly jump to any tab, switch themes, or start a new search from anywhere in the app.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts (`?`)**:
  - `/` — Jump straight to the search bar
  - `Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K` — Open the Quick Command Bar
  - `Ctrl+,` / `Cmd+,` — Open Settings
  - `Alt+1` through `Alt+6` — Switch between main app tabs
  - `Esc` — Close open menus or dialogs
- **Dark & Light Themes**: Switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode at any time in the header.
- **Personal Privacy Controls (`Settings`)**:
  - **Clear Search History**: Delete your recent search queries with one click.
  - **Clear History on Exit**: Automatically erase your search history whenever you close the browser tab.
  - **Blocked Websites List**: View and manage the list of websites you have chosen to hide from your search results.
